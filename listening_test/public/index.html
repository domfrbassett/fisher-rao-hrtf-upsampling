Before you begin

You will hear two brief sounds in each trial and decide which sound was farther left or higher up. Please complete the task in a quiet place using headphones.

Headphone and level check

Set the playback level to a comfortable, clearly audible volume. Press the check button as many times as needed. You should hear the first sound on the left and the second sound on the right. Do not change the volume after beginning the task.

Instructions

Each trial:

  1. Press Play trial.
  2. Listen to A and B.
  3. Choose A or B.

Respond according to perceived location, not sound quality or timbre. During the task there is no replay and no feedback.
